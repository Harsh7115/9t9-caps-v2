OUR PHILOSOPHY.

BUILT IN SILENCE.

We believe distinction does not need to announce itself.

It is found in the details, in the construction, in the design and in the intention behind every piece.

9T9 approaches headwear with an independent perspective, creating distinctive pieces that represent individuality, confidence and self-expression.

Not made to follow noise. Made to stand apart.

PACKAGING THAT STAYS.

DESIGNED TO DISPLAY. BUILT TO BE REUSED.

The custom printed card, front and rear.

More than a first impression.

Every 9T9 cap arrives in custom-designed cardboard packaging paired with a transparent reusable blister.

Designed to display and protect your cap, the blister can be reused multiple times for convenient storage.

An integrated hanging hole provides an additional way to display or store your packaged cap.

Distinctive by design. Practical by nature.
